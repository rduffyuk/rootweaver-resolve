Limitations: historical Ultra outputs overclaimed EndpointSlice semantics, contradicted hypothesis polarity, requested logs already supplied, and gave weak DNS explanations. An evidence-window flaw and simultaneous DNS/log enrichment prevent clean causal comparison. These failures are preserved, not silently rewritten. New integration requests use a changed prompt and are not proof of a statistically reliable repair.

For the platform, clearer capability/limit discovery and consistent usage/truncation diagnostics would reduce integration uncertainty. The unsent NetworkPolicy request was blocked by our local size guard, not by a provider outage.

No controlled cross-vendor or cloud/local benchmark, MTTR benefit or production qualification is claimed. Model quality and Nebius service behaviour are separate questions. Subjective ratings require the owner's review.
